**Novelty & Contribution:**
While existing literature extensively covers precision agriculture (e.g., crop recommendation) and economic forecasting (e.g., demand prediction) as isolated systems, our research bridges the gap between agronomic viability and economic reality. We present a novel architecture that integrates these predictive machine learning models directly into an e-commerce seller workflow. Using a 5-fold cross-validated Random Forest approach, our system achieves high accuracy for crop recommendations based on soil parameters (Accuracy = 99.54%) and actively forecasts demand using historical data. Furthermore, we conducted an ablation study demonstrating the limitations of current datasets for dynamic pricing in agriculture, highlighting critical areas for future exogenous data integration. An empirical user study involving 15 farmers and consumers achieved a System Usability Scale (SUS) score of 82.67, validating the system's practical learnability. We believe these findings directly align with the scope of *Computers and Electronics in Agriculture*, offering both a reproducible architectural blueprint and empirical insights into AgriTech supply chain integration.
