**Novelty & Contribution:**
While existing literature extensively covers precision agriculture (e.g., crop recommendation) and economic forecasting (e.g., demand prediction) as isolated systems, our research bridges the gap between agronomic viability and economic reality. We present a novel architecture that integrates these predictive machine learning models directly into an e-commerce seller workflow. Using a 5-fold cross-validated Random Forest approach, our system achieves high accuracy for crop recommendations based on soil parameters (Accuracy = 98.05%) and actively forecasts demand using historical data. Furthermore, we incorporated a risk-aware multi-objective optimizer for sustainable crop recommendations and conducted a climate-coupled ablation study demonstrating the value of integrating meteorological data into dynamic pricing models. An empirical user study involving 34 farmers and consumers achieved a System Usability Scale (SUS) score of 74.41, validating the system's practical learnability. We believe these findings directly align with the scope of *Computers and Electronics in Agriculture*, offering both a reproducible architectural blueprint and empirical insights into AgriTech supply chain integration.
