## Protocol
- **Target Audience:** Real users of the platform (N=15 pilot). 50% farmers, 50% consumers.
- **Execution:** Do not synthesize results. Execute the survey via Google Forms or paper copies.
- **Scoring:** Enter the raw scores (1-5) into the arrays inside `sus_score.py` and run it to compute the final standardized score (0-100) and grade.
